# ADR-001: Monolithic Branded House Architecture Model

**Status:** Approved & Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Deciders:** Business Owner, Lead Strategist  
**Decision ID:** BRAND-020  

---

## Context

The business is expanding beyond pure infant products into home essentials (cookware, organization) and bridal trousseau bundles. We needed to choose between three brand architecture models:
1. **House of Brands:** Separate brand identities and storefronts for Kids, Home, and Bridal.
2. **Endorsed Brands:** Distinct sub-brands strongly endorsed by a master entity (e.g., "Kids by Raghad").
3. **Monolithic Branded House:** A single master brand umbrella (**رغد | Raghad**) hosting all three pillars on one unified domain (`raghadworld.com`), with one cart, one customer support line, and shared customer lifetime value (LTV).

---

## Decision

We adopt the **Monolithic Branded House** model under the unified brand identity **رغد | Raghad**.

---

## Rationale

1. **Customer Lifecycle Progression (LTV Optimization):** Egyptian women naturally progress from bride-to-be ("جهاز العروسة") to young mother. A single domain allows seamless customer retention across life stages without re-acquisition costs.
2. **Capital Efficiency:** Eliminates fragmented Meta/TikTok marketing budgets across multiple domains and stores.
3. **Trust Transfer:** Trust earned in one pillar (e.g., cookware durability or infant safety) immediately transfers to the other pillars.

---

## Consequences

### Positive:
- Single cart and checkout experience increases Average Order Value (AOV).
- Centralized customer support via single WhatsApp business line.
- Unified SEO domain authority on `raghadworld.com`.

### Negative / Challenges:
- UX must carefully avoid "cluttered general store" perception through strict pillar navigation and curation filters.
