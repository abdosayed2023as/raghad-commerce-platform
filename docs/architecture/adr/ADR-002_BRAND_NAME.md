# ADR-002: Master Brand Naming Strategy ("رغد | Raghad")

**Status:** Approved & Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Deciders:** Business Owner, Lead Strategist  
**Decision ID:** BRAND-013, BRAND-019  

---

## Context

The previous technical draft used the legacy placeholder name "عالم رغد | Raghad World". Strategic analysis revealed that adding "World" was redundant, diluted modern luxury positioning, and weakened brand recall in Arabic conversational word-of-mouth.

---

## Decision

Adopt **رغد | Raghad** as the official, clean master brand name. Discard "Raghad World".

---

## Rationale

1. **Linguistic Depth:** In Arabic, "رغد" (Raghad) signifies abundance, ease of living, comfort, and peace of mind (*سعة العيش ورغد الحياة*). It directly mirrors the emotional benefit provided to Egyptian mothers and brides.
2. **Simplicity & Prestige:** Clean single-word names project premium enterprise authority.
3. **Conversational Fit:** Fits naturally into Egyptian conversational speech ("اشتريت من رغد").

---

## Consequences

### Positive:
- Higher memorability and stronger brand equity.
- Cleaner visual typography for logo identity and packaging.

### Negative / Challenges:
- Requires trademark registration for "رغد" across class categories 35 (E-commerce), 21 (Home), and 12/24 (Textiles/Kids).
