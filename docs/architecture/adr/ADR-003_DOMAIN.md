# ADR-003: Commercial Domain Strategy (`raghadworld.com` & `raghad.eg`)

**Status:** Approved & Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Deciders:** Business Owner, Lead Strategist  
**Decision ID:** BRAND-017, BRAND-019  

---

## Context

Securing domain infrastructure in Egypt requires balancing global commercial accessibility (`.com`) with local official legitimacy (`.eg`).

---

## Decision

1. Primary Global Commercial Domain: `raghadworld.com`
2. Official Egyptian Country TLD: `raghad.eg` (to be registered upon completion of commercial register procedures).

---

## Rationale

- `.com` provides immediate international platform readiness and universal trust.
- `.eg` provides localized Egyptian regulatory trust and legal protection against domain squatting.

---

## Consequences

### Positive:
- Protects digital intellectual property in Egypt.
- Allows seamless 301 redirects from `raghad.eg` to `raghadworld.com`.

### Negative / Challenges:
- Maintenance cost of managing multiple TLD DNS records.
