# ADR-004: Brand Tagline & Dual-Layer Promise

**Status:** Approved & Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Deciders:** Business Owner, Lead Strategist  
**Decision ID:** BRAND-013  

---

## Context

Egyptian e-commerce suffers from widespread consumer mistrust due to fraudulent product photos, low-quality reseller items, and poor post-purchase service. The brand tagline needed to immediately communicate absolute peace of mind in high-converting Egyptian Arabic.

---

## Decision

- **Main Tagline:** *"رغد... هتشتري وأنتي مغمضة"*  
- **Sub-Promise:** *"صورة حقيقية 100% | معاينة كاملة قبل الدفع | جودة تعيش معكِ ومع عائلتكِ"*

---

## Rationale

1. **Cultural Idiom:** "هتشتري وأنتي مغمضة" (Shop with your eyes closed) is a culturally rooted Egyptian phrase representing total, unconditional trust.
2. **Direct Risk Reversal:** Pairs emotional security with tangible operational guarantees (inspection before payment + HD real product clips).

---

## Consequences

### Positive:
- Instant competitive differentiation against drop-shippers and unverified sellers.
- Serves as the guiding compass for all customer support and logistics policies.

### Negative / Challenges:
- Imposes zero-tolerance standards on operations (every order must be inspected prior to dispatch).
