# ADR-005: 100% Real Photography & HD Video Media Policy

**Status:** Approved & Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Deciders:** Business Owner, Lead Strategist  
**Decision ID:** BRAND-004  

---

## Context

Many e-commerce competitors rely heavily on AI-generated product mockups, heavy studio filters, or stolen factory stock photos. When customers receive the real item, an "expectation vs. reality gap" occurs, causing high return rates and brand erosion.

---

## Decision

Enforce a strict **100% Real Product Media Mandate**:
- All product detail pages (PDPs) must feature 10-second unedited HD real video clips.
- Zero AI-generated product imagery or deceptive filter touch-ups allowed.

---

## Rationale

- Real media builds undeniable proof of product material, color accuracy, and build quality.
- Directly supports the pre-payment inspection conversion model.

---

## Consequences

### Positive:
- Drastically reduces return rate due to product mismatches.
- Enhances customer trust and repeat purchase rates.

### Negative / Challenges:
- Operational bottleneck: Requires media creation for every single SKU batch received at fulfillment centers.
