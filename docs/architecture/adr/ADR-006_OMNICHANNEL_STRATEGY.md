# ADR-006: Omnichannel Retail Expansion Strategy (7-Year Vision)

**Status:** Approved & Locked  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Deciders:** Business Owner, Lead Strategist  
**Decision ID:** BRAND-006  

---

## Context

Pure-play e-commerce in Egypt faces ceiling effects on customer trust and brand prestige if absent from physical retail touchpoints.

---

## Decision

Set a 7-year strategic milestone to evolve from a digital-first platform into an **omnichannel experience leader**, pairing the digital store (`raghadworld.com`) with flagship physical experience showrooms in Greater Cairo and key governorates.

---

## Rationale

Physical showrooms allow brides and mothers to experience product curation firsthand, serving as trust anchors and fulfillment hubs for online operations.

---

## Consequences

### Positive:
- Positions Raghad as a top-tier retail enterprise rather than a transient online store.
- Enables local pickup and physical product consultation.

### Negative / Challenges:
- Capital expenditure requirements for physical real estate expansion (deferred to post-profitability phase).
