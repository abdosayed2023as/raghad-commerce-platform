# Phase 01: Business Discovery

**Status:** Completed & Approved  
**Completion Date:** 2026-08-02  

---

## 1. Objectives
Examine the operational reality, supply chain, margins, sales channels, and underlying business mechanics of Raghad.

## 2. Questions Asked & Founder Answers
- **Q1:** What is the actual operational business of Raghad today?
  - *Answer:* Currently selling newborn baby supplies (up to 3 years old), primarily Chicco care sets & feeding gear, knee pads, bottles. Operating on EasyOrders + Facebook (1.5k followers) + Instagram + WhatsApp via Meta Conversion Ads. Sourcing from local importers/wholesalers.
- **Q2:** What are the margins and competitive advantage?
  - *Answer:* Gross margin per unit during ad campaigns is approx. 38% (before ad spend and fulfillment costs). Strategy relies on undercutting prices compared to pharmacies/big stores.
- **Q3:** How do you plan to expand to Home & Bridal with a 38% margin?
  - *Answer:* Expansion is enabled by a strategic partnership with a relative who is a direct importer of home & bridal supplies based in Mit El-Khouli Abdalla (Damietta). The partner supplies stock, shares fulfillment/shipping, and splits costs.
- **Q4:** What is the quality tier of the partner's inventory?
  - *Answer:* High quality, original premium brands (e.g., Neoflam cookware) and high-grade imports. Founder retains full curation rights over what enters Raghad.

## 3. Senior Consultant Analysis & Conclusions
- **Supply Moat Established:** The partnership with a Mit El-Khouli importer solves the capital lock-up risk and provides direct factory-level pricing.
- **Curation Control:** Curation authority ensures Raghad avoids becoming a cheap general store, protecting brand equity and raising Average Order Value (AOV).
