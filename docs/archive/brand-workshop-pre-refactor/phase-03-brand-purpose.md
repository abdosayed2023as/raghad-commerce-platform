# Phase 03: Brand Purpose

**Status:** Completed & Approved  
**Completion Date:** 2026-08-03  

---

## 1. Objectives
Define Raghad's core reason for existence beyond making money (The Simon Sinek "Why").

## 2. Questions Asked & Founder Answers
- **Q1:** If Raghad disappeared tomorrow, what would customers miss that no other store provides?
  - *Answer:* Customers would feel a lack of trust and have to restart their search journey to find a place offering genuine trust, quality, and honesty.
- **Q2:** What specific customer suffering/pain point does Raghad eliminate?
  - *Answer:* Online: Eliminates the "Product does not match the picture" expectation gap. Physical Retail: Eliminates customer exhaustion from searching across dozens of stores.

## 3. Senior Consultant Analysis & Approved Purpose Statement
- **Approved Brand Purpose Statement:**
  > **"تأسست 'رغد' لتكون الملاذ الآمن والوجهة الموثوقة للمرأة المصرية؛ لتلغي تماماً الخوف من الخديعة الإلكترونية وتنهي التشتت بين المتاجر، بتوفير مستلزمات حقيقية، صادقة، وتكاملية ترافِقها في تجهيز بيتها ورعاية طفلها بلا مفاجآت."**
