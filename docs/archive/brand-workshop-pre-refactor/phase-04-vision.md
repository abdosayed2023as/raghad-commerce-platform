**Status:** Completed & Approved  
**Completion Date:** 2026-08-03

## 1. Objectives
Define Raghad's 7-year future state, market positioning scale, omnichannel retail footprint, and industry benchmark ("Golden Standard").

## 2. Questions Asked & Founder Answers
- **Q1:** How will Raghad look in 7 years when it achieves full success, and what is its Golden Standard?
  - *Answer:* The #1 trusted E-commerce platform in Egypt alongside a network of flagship experience stores (فروع تجريبية / Experience Showrooms) across key governorates. The "Golden Standard" set for the market is Absolute Transparency & Quality ("الشفافية والجودة").

## 3. Senior Consultant Analysis & Approved Vision Statement
- **Strategic Analysis:** Showrooms in key governorates act as "Trust & Experience Anchors" reinforcing online conversions. Setting "Absolute Transparency & Quality" as the market standard positions Raghad as the pioneer defining benchmark expectations for Egyptian consumers.
- **Approved Vision Statement:**
  > **"أن نصبح خلال 7 سنوات الوجهة الإلكترونية والتجربة العائلية الأولى الأكثر ثقة في مصر، عبر المنصة الرائدة وسلسلة الفروع التفاعلية التي تُرسي المعيار الذهبي للشفافية والجودة، وتلغي الخديعة من تجربة الشراء المصرية."**
