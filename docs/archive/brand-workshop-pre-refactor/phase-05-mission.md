## 1. Objectives
Define Raghad's daily operational commitments, service deliverables, execution standards, and how it fulfills its Purpose and Vision for Egyptian women.

## 2. Questions Asked & Founder Answers
- **Q1:** What are Raghad's daily operational commitments regarding product selection, photography, quality inspection, and customer service?
  - *Answer:* 
    1. *Product Selection:* Clear standards prioritizing high quality and practical utility in solving customer problems.
    2. *Photography:* High-resolution real product photos from angles showing exact true details and textures.
    3. *Quality Control:* Mandatory pre-shipment inspection of every order to verify 100% item match and ensure zero defects (no cracks, broken parts, or damage).

## 3. Senior Consultant Analysis & Approved Mission Statement
- **Strategic Analysis:** Translates the Brand Purpose & Vision into 3 operational pillars: Practical Curation, HD Real Photography, and Zero-Defect Inspection.
- **Approved Mission Statement:**
  > **"مهمتنا اليومية في 'رغد' هي تقديم تجربة تسوق موثوقة وعملية للمرأة المصرية، عبر انتقاء منتجات عالية الجودة تحل مشكلاتها الواقعية، وتوثيقها بصرياً بدقة وصدق تام، وخضوع كل طلب لفحص دقيق ومباشر قبل الشحن لضمان سلامته ومطابقته المطلقة."**
