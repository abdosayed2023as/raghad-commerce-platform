## 1. Objectives
Define Raghad's precise target buyer personas, demographics, socioeconomic status, geographic density, buying habits, and primary decision drivers in Egypt.

## 2. Approved Target Personas

### Persona 1: The Young Mother (الأم الشابة)
- **Age Range:** 20 - 30 years old.
- **Life Stage:** First-time mother (or 2nd infant).
- **Geographic Density:** Greater Cairo (Cairo & Giza primary), followed by Delta & key governorates.
- **Socioeconomic Status:** Mid-Class to Upper-Mid Class (B / B+).
- **Primary Buying Driver:** Quality & Authenticity ("الجودة والأصالة") due to heightened protective instincts regarding infant health and safety.

### Persona 2: The Bride-to-be & Her Mother (العروسة وأمها)
- **Age Range:** 18 - 27 years old (shopping with her mother/parents).
- **Life Stage:** Marital home preparation ("تجهيز شقة الزوجية").
- **Socioeconomic Status:** Mid-Class to Upper-Mid Class (B / B+).
- **Primary Buying Drivers:** 
  - *The Bride:* Visual prestige, modern aesthetics, and elegance ("الشياكة والفخامة أمام الأهل والضيوف").
  - *The Mother:* Material durability, original brand authenticity, and practical utility.
  - *Combined Hook:* Sleek modern design + 100% original quality guarantee + bundled trousseau convenience.
