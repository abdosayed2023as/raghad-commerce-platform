# Phase 08: Customer Psychology

**Status:** Completed & Approved  
**Completion Date:** 2026-08-03  

---

## 1. Objectives
Identify the emotional triggers, psychological fears, hesitations, guilt factors, and cognitive biases influencing Egyptian women at the exact moment of checkout.

## 2. Core Conversion Barrier Identified
- **Primary Checkout Barrier:** Fear of being scammed or receiving inferior/mismatched quality ("الخوف من النصب والغش وعدم الوثوق في الجودة").

## 3. Approved Risk Reversal Framework (درع إزالة المخاطر)

1. **حق المعاينة الكاملة قبل الدفع (Inspection Before Cash Payment):**
   - Customer inspects the physical product in front of the courier before paying.

2. **فيديو الواقعية المباشر (HD Real-Product Video Previews):**
   - 10-second unedited real video clip on PDP showing true texture, weight, and color without filters.

3. **ضمان الاستبدال الفوري المجاني (Zero-Cost Instant Replacement):**
   - Free doorstep replacement within 48 hours for any manufacturing defect ("العميل لا يدفع ثمن خطئنا").
