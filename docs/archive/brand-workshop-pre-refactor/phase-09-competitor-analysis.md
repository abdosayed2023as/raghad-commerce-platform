## 1. Objectives
Map the competitive landscape in Egypt across baby supplies, home goods, and bridal gear, identifying direct/indirect competitors, market gaps, and Raghad's differentiation leverage.

## 2. Competitive Landscape & Vulnerability Matrix

| Competitor Cluster | Key Players | Competitor Vulnerabilities | Raghad's Strategic Counter-Strike |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Specialty Baby Stores** | Raya Store, CocoBe, Kids Mart, MamiApp | Heavy reliance on AI-generated renders/photos that artificially beautify products, creating an expectation gap. | **100% Real HD Video Previews:** Zero AI trickery; unedited real product textures & colors. |
| **Drop-Shippers & Marketplaces** | Taager affiliate sellers, Amazon.eg sellers, Noon.com sellers | Anonymous sellers, fake reviews, zero pre-payment inspection, high product mismatch rate. | **Mandatory Inspection Before Payment:** 100% cash-on-delivery inspection before paying 1 EGP. |
| **Mass Discount Retailers** | Raneen (رنين) | Overwhelming physical chaos, low perceived quality, zero curation, tiring shopping experience. | **Curated Premium Tier (Neoflam standard):** One-stop curated elegance without physical fatigue. |
