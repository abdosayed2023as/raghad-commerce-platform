## 1. Objectives
Define Raghad's human archetype, behavioral traits, tone character, and practical interaction rules with customers to eliminate vague clichés.

## 2. Approved Brand Archetype: The Wise Older Sister (الأخت الكبرى الخبيرة والموثوقة)

### Why this archetype works:
- **For the Bride (18-27):** Acts as the stylish, experienced older sister who has already been through marital home prep and knows which cookware & organizers are genuine quality vs cheap knockoffs.
- **For the Young Mother (20-30):** Acts as the protective, experienced mother-figure/sister who knows which infant care tools & feeding gear are genuinely safe and practical.

### Core Character Traits:
1. **صريحة وصادقة (Candid & Honest):** Gives direct, truthful advice; never pushes unnecessary items just to make a sale.
2. **ذات ذوق رفيع (Refined & Stylish):** Curates modern, elegant visual aesthetics (Neoflam level).
3. **حريصة ومجربة (Experienced & Protective):** Protects her younger sisters (Brides & Mothers) from scams, mismatched photos, and low quality.
4. **مطمئنة وغير ملحة (Calming & Non-Pushy):** Creates a zero-anxiety, transparent shopping experience.
