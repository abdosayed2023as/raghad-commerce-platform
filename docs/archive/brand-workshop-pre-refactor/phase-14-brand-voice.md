**Status:** Completed & Approved

## 1. Objectives
Establish Raghad's tone of voice, linguistic parameters, Do's and Don'ts, and real-life copywriting examples across marketing, customer support, and website copy.

## 2. Approved Brand Voice Framework

- **Tone Character:** Warm, Refined, Honest, Contemporary Respectful Egyptian (دافئة، راقية، مصري معاصر محترم).
- **Archetype Alignment:** Speaks with the calm authority and genuine care of "The Wise Older Sister" (الأخت الكبرى الخبيرة).

### Do's & Don'ts Rules:
1. **DO:** Use respectful, warm Egyptian phrasing ("نورتينا", "بيتك وزوجيتك", "معاينة كاملة على الطبيعة").
2. **DO:** Focus on clear value & benefit over pushy sales tactics.
3. **DON'T:** Use cheap slang hype or pushy sales gimmicks ("لحق العرض المجنون", "اقوى خصم").
4. **DON'T:** Use overly stiff/formal classical Arabic that feels cold or distant.
