# Phase 16: Brand Messaging Framework

**Status:** Completed & Approved  
**Completion Date:** 2026-08-03  

---

## 1. Objectives
Formulate Raghad's specific marketing messaging pillars, ad copy angles, and value headlines tailored to the 3 sub-pillars (Raghad Kids, Raghad Home, Raghad Bridal).

## 2. Approved 3-Pillar Messaging Architecture (Refined)

### 1. رغد كيدز (Raghad Kids)
- **Primary Campaign Headline:** **"لأن أمان طفلك مش مجال للتجربة"**
- **Ad Copy Angle:** Focus on 100% genuine infant care products (Chicco standard), child health safety, real HD video previews, and pre-payment doorstep inspection.

### 2. رغد هوم (Raghad Home)
- **Primary Campaign Headline:** **"بيتك شيك وخامته تعيش العمر"**
- **Ad Copy Angle:** Focus on Neoflam-tier premium cookware, home decor, and household organizers sourced directly from Damietta importers at direct factory prices without middleman markups.

### 3. رغد العروسة (Raghad Bridal)
- **Primary Campaign Headline:** **"تجهيز بيتك من غير ما تلفي ولا تحتاري"**
- **Ad Copy Angle:** Focus on modern visual prestige ("الشياكة والفخامة أمام الضيوف") combined with material durability that reassures the mother, packaged in curated trousseau bundles that eliminate store fragmentation.
