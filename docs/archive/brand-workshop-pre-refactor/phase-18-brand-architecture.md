# Phase 18: Brand Architecture

**Status:** Completed & Approved  
**Completion Date:** 2026-08-03

## 1. Objectives
Define Raghad's brand architecture model (Branded House vs House of Brands), sub-pillar relationship, category navigation tree, and customer lifecycle cross-selling structure.

## 2. Approved Model: Monolithic Branded House (نموذج البيت الموحد)

### Architecture Framework:
- **Master Brand:** **رغد | Raghad**
- **Domain:** `raghadworld.com`
- **Sub-Pillars (Inside 1 Unified Platform):**
  1. **رغد كيدز (Raghad Kids)**
  2. **رغد هوم (Raghad Home)**
  3. **رغد العروسة (Raghad Bridal)**

### Strategic Benefits:
- **Single Cart & Checkout:** Unified customer account, single cart drawer, and 1 WhatsApp support line across all 3 pillars.
- **Maximized Lifetime Value (LTV):** Seamless cross-selling as a woman transitions from Bride (Bridal prep) -> Homemaker (Home upgrade) -> Mother (Infant care).
- **Reduced CAC:** Marketing efforts build cumulative equity under one domain instead of diluting ad spend across fragmented sub-brands.
