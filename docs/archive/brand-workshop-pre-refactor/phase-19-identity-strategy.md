# Phase 19: Identity Strategy (Visual Directives)

**Status:** Completed & Approved  
**Completion Date:** 2026-08-03  

---

## 1. Objectives
Establish the strategic visual identity directives, primary signature brand color guidelines, typography parameters, iconography standards, and visual design system criteria before visual asset creation.

## 2. Approved Visual Identity Directives

### 1. Emotional Visual Impression
- **Core Aesthetic:** Warm Modern Elegance & Calming Luxury (الأناقة الدافئة العصرية، الفخامة المريحة غير المتكلفة، والهدوء الذي يبعث على الثقة).

### 2. Signature Color Palette Parameters
- **Primary Accent (اللون التوقيعي الرئيسي):** Warm Terracotta / Rose Gold (`#D48C80`) — Conveys feminine warmth, family comfort, and modern elegance.
- **Secondary Contrast (اللون الثانوي للتباين والقيادة):** Premium Slate Plum (`#2C2230`) — Gives sharp visual contrast, luxury depth, and readability for CTA buttons and primary headlines.
- **Surface Canvas (لون الخلفية):** Warm Silk White (`#FAFAFA`) — Clean, non-glare, non-distracting background that highlights real product photography.
- **Trust & Alert Utility Colors:** Forest Emerald (`#10B981`) for zero-risk inspection badges; Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`) for soft deposit alerts.

### 3. Typography Directives
- **Primary Arabic Typeface:** Cairo (Weights: 500 Medium for body, 700 Bold for headings, 800 Extra Bold for prices & primary CTAs). Clean geometric proportions with zero traditional clutter.

### 4. Iconography & Media Standards
- **Icons:** Custom 2px stroke geometric vector icons (replacing cheap emojis).
- **Product Media:** 100% Real HD Photography & unedited 10-second Real Video clips (Zero deceptive AI mockups).
