## 1. Active Assumptions Pending Empirical Validation

### A-001: In-House Real Content Production Capacity
- **Assumption:** The operational team can produce high-quality real photography and 10-second unedited video clips for all incoming inventory from Damietta without creating a shipping or listing bottleneck.
- **Validation Method:** Conduct a pilot batch shoot of 20 new items to measure turnaround time and cost per listing.
- **Status:** Pending Operational Validation.

### A-002: Multi-Pillar Navigation Harmony
- **Assumption:** Egyptian mothers and brides can navigate Kids, Home, and Bridal within a single digital domain (`raghadworld.com`) without experiencing cognitive dissonance or feeling the store lacks specialization.
- **Validation Method:** Wireframe user testing during UI prototyping and UX design phase.
- **Status:** Validated in Phase 18 (Branded House Model). UX prototype testing pending.

### A-003: Partner Logistics Competency
- **Assumption:** The importer partner in Mit El-Khouli Abdalla (Damietta) can maintain e-commerce level packaging standards, quick dispatch, and handle customer return inspections seamlessly.
- **Validation Method:** Audit partner's warehouse fulfillment process and run trial shipments.
- **Status:** Pending Operational Validation.
