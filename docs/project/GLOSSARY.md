## Part A — Brand & Business Terms

### Arabic Brand Vocabulary

- **رغد | Raghad** — The master brand name. "Raghad" means *abundance, ease of living, and peace of mind* in Arabic — chosen to evoke the emotional promise of the brand.
- **رغد كيدز (Raghad Kids)** — The Kids pillar; newborn care and infant gear (0–3 years).
- **رغد هوم (Raghad Home)** — The Home pillar; premium cookware and home organizers.
- **رغد العروسة (Raghad Bridal)** — The Bridal pillar; curated trousseau bundles and wedding prep essentials.
- **"رغد... هتشتري وأنتي مغمضة"** — The main brand tagline. Literal translation: *"Raghad… you'll shop with your eyes closed."* Meaning: the brand guarantees 100% trust, so you don't need to worry about what you're buying.
- **"الشياكة"** — Elegance/stylishness; the primary emotional driver for the Bride persona.
- **"الأخت الكبرى الخبيرة والموثوقة"** — "The Wise Older Sister" — the approved brand archetype. Candid, stylish, protective, and non-pushy.
- **"عالم رغد | Raghad World"** — ⚠️ LEGACY NAME. Pre-workshop brand identity. Superseded by **رغد | Raghad**. Never use in new documentation.
- **"جهاز العروسة"** — The traditional Egyptian trousseau; the complete set of household items a bride prepares before marriage.

### Strategic Brand Terms

- **Brand Purpose (Simon Sinek WHY)** — The foundational reason the brand exists: eliminating online deception for Egyptian women.
- **Brand Vision** — The 7-year strategic aspiration: becoming Egypt's #1 trusted e-commerce platform with physical showrooms.
- **Brand Mission** — The daily operational commitments: practical curation, HD real media, and pre-shipment QC.
- **Brand Archetype** — A universal personality framework applied to brand identity. Raghad's archetype: The Wise Older Sister.
- **Brand Architecture** — The structural model governing how brand pillars relate. Raghad uses the **Monolithic Branded House** model.
- **Monolithic Branded House** — A brand architecture where all sub-categories (Kids, Home, Bridal) operate under one master brand, one domain, one checkout, and one support line.
- **UVP (Unique Value Proposition)** — Raghad's 3-pillar UVP: [Expert Curation (Neoflam/Chicco standard)] + [Fair Pricing (direct sourcing from Mit El-Khouli)] + [Zero-Risk Experience].
- **Brand Promise** — The commitment every touchpoint must deliver. Raghad's promise: *"صورة حقيقية 100% | معاينة كاملة قبل الدفع | جودة تعيش معكِ ومع عائلتكِ"*
- **Brand Voice** — The linguistic personality of the brand. Raghad: warm, refined, honest, contemporary Egyptian Arabic (*عامية فاخرة ودافئة*).
- **Brand Memory** — The consolidated single-page brand DNA document (`06_BRAND_MEMORY.md`). Designed for fast onboarding.

### Personas & Audience

- **Dual-Persona Strategy** — Raghad targets two segments simultaneously: Young Mothers (20–30) and Brides-to-be with their Mothers (18–27).
- **Persona 1: الأم الشابة (The Young Mother)** — Primary driver for Raghad Kids. Focus: infant health safety and product authenticity.
- **Persona 2: العروسة وأمها (The Bride-to-be & Her Mother)** — Primary driver for Raghad Bridal/Home. The Bride seeks elegance; her Mother seeks durability and authenticity.
- **Customer LTV (Lifecycle Leverage)** — Brides naturally graduate into the Young Mother persona, extending their lifetime value within the Raghad ecosystem.

### Competitive & CRO Terms

- **Risk Reversal Framework (درع الثقة الثلاثي)** — Raghad's 3-step conversion framework: (1) Pre-payment doorstep inspection, (2) HD real video clips, (3) Free 48-hour replacement guarantee.
- **Pre-payment Inspection** — Customers open and verify packages before handing cash to the delivery driver. Directly neutralizes Egypt's #1 e-commerce barrier: fear of scams.
- **Real Product Media Mandate** — Approved by founder (BRAND-004). All product visuals must be 100% real, unedited photography and HD video. Zero AI-generated or filter-enhanced images.
- **Counter-Strike Matrix** — The competitive differentiation strategy mapping Raghad's advantages against specific competitor weaknesses (vs. Amazon/Noon, vs. Raya Store, vs. Raneen).
- **CAC** — Customer Acquisition Cost; the marketing spend to acquire one new customer.
- **Golden Standard of Transparency** — Raghad's stated market aspiration: to define the industry benchmark for product honesty and visual accuracy in Egyptian e-commerce.

### Supply Chain Terms

- **Mit El-Khouli Abdalla (ميت الخولي عبدالله، دمياط)** — Egypt's primary wholesale hub for home and bridal goods, located in Damietta. Site of Raghad's direct factory sourcing partnership — the brand's key COGS moat.
- **COGS** — Cost of Goods Sold; the direct cost to produce or procure a sold product.
- **Neoflam Standard** — The quality benchmark for Raghad Home products. Neoflam is a premium cookware brand; using its quality tier as a minimum acceptable standard signals premium curation.
- **Chicco Standard** — The quality benchmark for Raghad Kids products. Chicco is a leading infant brand; using its tier signals safety and authenticity.
- **Founder Curation Right (BRAND-003)** — The founder holds an explicit, non-negotiable veto right over any incoming product batch that fails to meet Raghad's quality standards.

## Part B — Project & Governance Terms

- **RCP** — Raghad Commerce Platform; the official name of this project repository.
- **Business Owner** — The role responsible for overall business vision, strategic approval, and brand alignment. Never identified by personal name in documentation.
- **Product Architect** — The role responsible for repository architecture, workflow enforcement, and technical governance.
- **Documentation Owner** — The role responsible for documentation standards, cross-link integrity, and SSOT enforcement.
- **Implementation Engineer** — The role responsible for theme development and custom component execution.
- **Code Reviewer** — The role responsible for code quality, QA, and specification compliance validation.
- **SSOT (Single Source of Truth)** — The definitive, undisputed reference for a given fact. In this project, the repository is the SSOT.
- **TCP (Task Completion Protocol)** — The mandatory final phase of every task, requiring documentation impact assessment, synchronization, and knowledge registration before task closure.
- **RFC (Request for Comments)** — A formal proposal for a change to architecture, governance, or major technical implementation. Follows a defined lifecycle: Draft → Review → Approved → Implemented → Verified → Closed.
- **ADR (Architecture Decision Record)** — A permanent technical record of choices made during a major technical RFC.
- **Framework Freeze (OPS-0007)** — An approved governance rule prohibiting introduction of new governance layers, workflow stages, or documentation systems unless a demonstrated operational problem exists.
- **KMS (Knowledge Management System)** — The system governing how research sessions are tracked, indexed, and cross-referenced (`research/KNOWLEDGE_INDEX.md`).
- **RLS (Reference Library System)** — The system of reusable reference documents in `research/reference/` containing lookup-ready technical facts.

## Part C — Technical Platform Terms

- **Easy Orders** — The Egyptian e-commerce SaaS platform on which the Raghad storefront is built.
- **Liquid** — The templating language used by Easy Orders (inherited from Shopify's Liquid). Used in theme templates, sections, and snippets.
- **Theme** — The complete Liquid/CSS/JS codebase representing the storefront's appearance and functionality.
- **Section** — A customizable, reusable content block within an Easy Orders theme.
- **Snippet** — A small, reusable code fragment included within sections or templates.
- **Template** — The Liquid file defining the layout structure of a specific page type (product, collection, cart, etc.).
- **UX** — User Experience; the overall flow and emotional quality of interaction.
- **UI** — User Interface; the visual components, layouts, and interactions.
- **CRO** — Conversion Rate Optimization; strategies to increase the percentage of visitors who complete a purchase.
- **SEO** — Search Engine Optimization; practices to improve organic search visibility.
- **PDP** — Product Details Page; the page showcasing a single product.
- **PLP** — Product Listing Page; a category or collection page showing multiple products.
- **AOV** — Average Order Value; the mean amount spent per completed checkout.
- **LTV** — Customer Lifetime Value; the total expected revenue from a single customer over their relationship with the brand.
- **Epic** — A large body of work broken into specific tasks or specifications.
- **Sprint** — A time-boxed iteration of focused project work.
- **QA** — Quality Assurance; the testing phase verifying acceptance criteria are met.
- **COD** — Cash on Delivery; the dominant payment model in Egyptian e-commerce.
- **Design Token** — A named, platform-agnostic variable representing a design decision (e.g., `color-primary: #D48C80`, `radius-card: 12px`).
- **Information Architecture** — The structural design and organization of content within a digital experience.
- **User Flow** — The path a user takes through the platform to complete a specific goal.
- **Product Card** — A UI component displaying a product summary (image, price, title, trust indicators).
- **Mobile First** — A design principle requiring all experiences to be designed for mobile users first, then enhanced for larger screens.
