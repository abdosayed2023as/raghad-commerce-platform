# Purpose

# Project Name

Raghad World

---

# Project Type

E-commerce Rebranding

Custom Theme Development for Easy Orders

Brand Identity

UX/UI Design

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

Design System

Scalable Commerce Platform

---

# Vision

Build one of the most trusted lifestyle brands for Egyptian families.

Raghad World starts with children's products and gradually expands into home essentials and bridal essentials while maintaining a consistent customer experience centered around trust.

---

# Mission

Create an online shopping experience where customers feel completely confident purchasing carefully selected products for their family.

Every product should reflect quality, transparency, and trust.

---

# Business Goals

Business priorities are ordered by importance.

1. Build a strong brand.
2. Increase conversion rate.
3. Increase sales.
4. Increase average order value (AOV).
5. Increase returning customers.

---

# Brand Philosophy

We never sell a product we don't trust.

This philosophy is non-negotiable.

Every product added to the store must satisfy the brand's quality standards.

---

# Core Brand Value

Trust.

Trust is the foundation of every customer interaction.

Design, content, customer service, marketing, and product presentation should all reinforce trust.

---

# Brand Personality

Raghad is:

- Friendly
- Kind
- Helpful
- Elegant
- Respectful
- Close to people
- Gives honest advice
- Wants the best for customers

Customers should feel they are talking to a trusted person rather than a sales representative.

---

# Brand Promise

Customers should feel:

"I can buy anything from Raghad with confidence."

---

# Target Audience

Current primary audience:

Parents of children aged 0–2 years.

Future expansion:

- Parents
- Families
- Brides
- Home shoppers

The architecture of the website must support future expansion from day one.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Raghad World is intended to become a complete family lifestyle brand rather than a children's store.

Future product categories include:

- Baby & Kids
- Home
- Bridal Essentials

Future services may include:

- Private Label
- Loyalty Program
- AI Shopping Assistant
- Mobile Application
- Educational Content
- Community Platform

---

# Design Philosophy

Beautiful design alone is not enough.

Every design decision must support one or more of the following:

- Build trust
- Reduce purchase anxiety
- Improve usability
- Increase conversions
- Support scalability

If a design element does not create business value, it should be questioned.

---

# Project Principles

- Strategy before Design
- Design before Code
- Official Easy Orders Documentation First
- Documentation before Implementation
- Evidence over Opinions
- Mobile First
- Performance Matters
- Accessibility Matters
- Reusable Components
- No Undocumented Easy Orders APIs

---

# Project Roles

The following official roles govern this project. Personal names are never used in documentation.

- **Business Owner**: Responsible for business vision, strategy, and ultimate approval of project decisions.
- **Product Architect**: Responsible for the repository architecture, workflow enforcement, and technical direction.
- **Documentation Owner**: Responsible for maintaining the documentation standards, templates, and ensuring synchronization.
- **Implementation Engineer**: Responsible for building features and themes exactly as specified.
- **Code Reviewer**: Responsible for reviewing code, QA, and validating specifications.
