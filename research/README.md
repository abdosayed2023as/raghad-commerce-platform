# Research Infrastructure

Version: 1.0.0
Status: Active
Owner: Documentation Engineer

## Purpose
This directory is the central hub for all research, audits, and discovery work for the Raghad World project. Research conducted here is exploratory and designed to validate assumptions before they become business decisions and official documentation.

## Research Lifecycle
Research follows a strict lifecycle:
1. **Research Notes**: Temporary notes taken during the session.
2. **Reviewed**: AI or peer review of findings and insights.
3. **Approved**: Accepted as valid insights.
4. **Official Documentation**: Findings are synthesized into official project documentation.

Research NEVER goes directly into official documentation.

## Folder Organization
- **`branding/`**: Brand strategy, identity, tone, and positioning.
- **`competitors/`**: Industry benchmarking and competitor analysis.
- **`easyorders/`**: Easy Orders platform constraints, themes, and APIs.
- **`market/`**: Target audience, psychology, and trends.
- **`website-audit/`**: UX, CRO, and performance audits.
- **`templates/`**: Reusable templates for research sessions.

## Naming Conventions
- **Directories**: `lower-kebab-case/`
- **Session Files**: `rs-XX-session-name.md` (e.g., `rs-01-documentation-map.md`)
- **Templates**: `UPPER_SNAKE_CASE.md` (e.g., `SESSION_TEMPLATE.md`)

## Session Workflow
1. Duplicate the `SESSION_TEMPLATE.md`.
2. Name it according to the naming conventions.
3. Conduct the research without making implementation decisions.
4. Conclude the session with observations, insights, and potential decisions.

## Difference Between Research Notes and Official Documentation
Research Notes are working files that capture raw findings, ideas, and open questions during the discovery phase. They are not official business decisions. Official Documentation represents the finalized, approved business rules, architecture, and specifications that govern implementation.
